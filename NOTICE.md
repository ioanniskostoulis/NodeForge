## Original work

The TypeScript application, the interface, the command console, the NodeForge engine (error-controlled stepping, SPICE-style solving and the analyses), the model library and the validation workflow are new code. The NodeForge engine builds on the port and is neither the ngspice nor the LTspice engine; the VDMOS model of ngspice has an LTspice lineage, and NodeForge follows the implementation of ngspice. `assets/models/generic.lib` holds generic models written for NodeForge, from which `public/models.json` is generated. Models imported by a user keep the terms of their authors.
